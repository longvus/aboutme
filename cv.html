Cost-Aware Stopping for Freeze–Thaw Hyperparameter Optimization: When Does One-Step Lookahead Suffice?
Jan 2026 – Present
  • Formulate freeze–thaw hyperparameter optimization as a cost-adjusted switching-and-stopping problem; derive a Bellman identity decomposing continuation value into one-step net Expected Improvement (EI) and a nonnegative downstream option value; prove EI stopping terminates and never underperforms stopping immediately.
  • Develop a certified two-step policy-improvement rule with rigorous Gaussian enclosures; use empirical evidence to quantify when one-step EI stopping is near-optimal and when deeper lookahead helps.
Expected-Improvement Stopping for Multi-Fidelity Bayesian Optimization: A Pandora’s Box Analysis
Jun 2025 – Present
  • Generalize Pandora's-box index theory to staged multi-fidelity evaluation with arbitrary collectable fidelities; derive a nested covered-call recursion and prove a reservation-index policy is Bellman-optimal when candidates are independent.
  • Characterize rung-wise EI stopping as a conservative index policy, derive an exact loss decomposition, and develop depth-k chase certificates that guarantee nondecreasing expected utility under a fixed surrogate; benchmark against index, knowledge-gradient, Hyperband, and successive-halving baselines.
